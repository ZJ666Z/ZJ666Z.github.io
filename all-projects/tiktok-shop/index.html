🔴 P0 - Problem 1🔴 P0 - 问题 1Pre-purchase购买前

(SEA & US) Clarify the differences between insurance and warranty（东南亚与美国）澄清保险与商家保修的区别

Context背景

Through a UX audit, I firstly identified a critical ambiguity where users struggle to distinguish between paid insurance and free warranty—an insight later validated by data showing a clear negative impact on conversion.我在体验走查中首先发现：用户难以区分付费保险与免费保修。后续数据进一步验证了这一认知混淆，并显示它会明显损害转化。

After aligning with stakeholders, the potential impact was recognized. However, due to the current lack of A/B testing capabilities, we reviewed the prioritization and ROI, deciding to schedule this optimization for Q4 2025.与利益相关方对齐后，团队认可了问题的潜在影响。由于当时缺少 A/B 测试能力，我们重新评估优先级与 ROI，决定将本次优化排入 2025 年第四季度。

Problem问题

🎨 Identify by UX🎨 体验走查发现

Cognitive Ambiguity认知混淆

  • Users struggle to distinguish between Platform Insurance (paid) and Seller Warranty (included).用户难以区分平台保险（付费）与商家保修（已包含）。
  • This ambiguity creates cognitive load, forcing users to investigate further or hesitate, rather than making a quick purchase decision.这种含糊增加了认知负担，用户需要继续查找信息或犹豫，而无法快速做出购买决定。

  • The data suggests users are staying longer due to confusion, not engagement.数据表明，用户 停留更久是因为困惑，而不是更投入。

Design Proposals设计方案

Option A: Minimal Effort - Copy Update Only方案 A：低投入——仅更新文案

✅ Pros:✅ 优点：

  • Preserves existing design: Maximizes the retention of current design decisions.保留现有设计： 最大限度保留当前设计决策。
  • Simple and straightforward: Maintains a minimalist UI without adding visual clutter.简单直接： 保持界面简洁，不增加视觉负担。

❌ Cons:❌ 缺点：

  • Too Conservative: The change might be too subtle.改动过于保守： 变化可能不够明显。
  • Habituation (Banner Blindness): Users, especially returning ones, often scan past text-heavy areas out of habit. A subtle copy update lacks the visual disruption needed to break this pattern and capture their attention.习惯性忽略（横幅盲区）： 用户，尤其是回访用户，常会习惯性略过文字密集区域。仅做轻微文案调整，难以打破这一浏览习惯并抓住注意。
  • Low Engagement: Relying solely on text (copy) runs the risk that users still won't read or process the information.参与度低： 只依赖文字，用户仍可能不阅读或无法有效理解。

Option B: Medium Effort - Visual Enhancement方案 B：中等投入——强化视觉表达

✅ Pros:✅ 优点：

  • Informative & Scannable: Provides sufficient information but in a more visual format compared to Option A.信息充分、易于扫读： 在信息充分的同时，比方案 A 更具视觉性。
  • Better Readability: Much easier for users to digest than a block of text.可读性更好： 相比大段文字更容易理解。

❌ Cons:❌ 缺点：

  • Development Cost: Requires building new UI components.开发成本： 需要开发新的 UI 组件。
  • Learning Curve: Potential friction if the new pattern is unfamiliar to users.学习成本： 如果用户不熟悉新模式，可能带来额外阻力。

Option C: High Effort - Comprehensive Data Table方案 C：高投入——完整对比表

✅ Pros:✅ 优点：

  • Highly Informative & Flexible: Offers maximum detail with the option to collapse.信息最完整且灵活： 提供最完整细节，并支持折叠。
  • Straightforward: The tabular format is easy to scan.直观： 表格形式便于快速扫读。

❌ Cons:❌ 缺点：

  • Cognitive Load: Risk of information overload.认知负担： 存在信息过载风险。
  • Intrusiveness: Could be perceived as annoying or distracting by users.干扰性： 用户可能觉得打扰或分散注意。

🔴 P0 - Problem 2🔴 P0 - 问题 2Post-purchase购买后

(SEA) Distinguish the Protection Policy and Insurance Claim Info（东南亚）区分保单与保险理赔信息

Context背景

Insurance information involves two distinct user-experience paths—policy status and claim status. Unlike us, competitors employ progressive disclosure to manage user attention and display these two paths separately.保险信息包含两条不同的用户路径：保单状态与理赔状态。竞品会通过渐进披露管理用户注意力，并将两条路径分开呈现。

After aligning with stakeholders, the proposal was well received. However, stakeholders consider improving insurance purchase flow with a higher priority than claims experience, so this optimization was scheduled for Q4 follow-up.与利益相关方对齐后，方案获得积极反馈；但团队认为购买流程优先级高于理赔体验，因此将本项优化安排在第四季度继续推进。

Post-pruchase Experience购买后体验

  • Policy: The contract you bought. It is the proof that you have protection (e.g., "Active" or "Expired").保单： 用户购买的保险合同，用于证明保障是否有效，例如“生效中”或“已到期”。
  • Claim: The request you make when something goes wrong. It is the process of asking the insurance company for money or repairs.理赔： 发生问题后用户提交的申请，用于向保险公司请求赔付或维修。

Problem问题

🎨 UX Identified🎨 体验走查发现

Ambiguous Information Architecture信息架构含糊

  • The user flows for Protection Policies and Insurance Claims are not clearly distinguished.保单与保险理赔的用户流程没有被清楚区分。
  • Confusing progress indicators fail to signal the independence of these two functions, creating a mismatch with the user's mental model.含糊的进度指示 无法说明两项功能彼此独立，与用户心智模型不一致。
👤 User Pain Point👤 用户痛点

Navigation Friction导航阻力

  • Users struggle to discern between managing a policy and tracking a claim due to the lack of separation in the interface.界面没有明确分区，用户难以判断自己是在管理保单，还是追踪理赔。
  • Policy: Processing → Protected → Expired; (Failed/Cancelled)保单： 处理中 → 保障中 → 已到期；（失败／已取消）
  • Claim: Claim in review → Review outcome (Approved/Rejected) → Resolution (Paid)理赔： 理赔审核中 → 审核结果（通过／拒绝）→ 处理完成（已赔付）

Impact影响

💼 Business Risk💼 业务风险

Operational Costs & Trust运营成本与信任

  • Increased Support Volume: Confusion when searching for claim progress leads to a spike in Customer Service (CS) inquiries.客服量增加： 用户查找理赔进度时产生困惑，导致客服咨询量上升。
  • Erosion of Trust: A confusing post-purchase experience damages brand credibility.信任受损： 含糊的购买后体验会损害品牌可信度。
📉 Long-term Metrics📉 长期指标

Key Data Metrics Decline关键指标下降

  • Risk of reducing Net Promoter Score (NPS) and, ultimately, Gross Written Premium (GWP) due to poor retention.较差的留存可能拉低净推荐值（NPS），并最终影响承保总额（GWP）。

How might we clearly distinguish the policy coverage flow from the insurance claim flow to prevent user confusion?我们如何清楚区分保单保障流程与保险理赔流程，避免用户混淆？

Opportunity机会

Separate the Paths分离两条路径

  • Create distinct pages for Policies and Claims. "Holding a policy" and "Tracking a refund" are different tasks and should not be mixed.为保单与理赔建立独立页面。“持有保单”和“追踪赔付”是不同任务，不应混在一起。

Visual Distinction视觉区分

Use different UI patterns to clarify the difference:用不同的界面模式说明差异：

  • Policies: Use static Status Labels (e.g., Active).保单： 使用静态状态标签，例如“生效中”。
  • Claims: Use dynamic Progress Bars (e.g., In Review → Paid).理赔： 使用动态进度条，例如“审核中 → 已赔付”。

Instant Clarity一眼看懂

Design Proposals设计方案

Design Rationale & Strategy设计依据与策略

Prioritizing by Frequency按使用频率确定优先级

  • Data-Driven: Existing data indicates that the "Claims" function has a very low usage rate.数据依据： 现有数据表明，“理赔”功能的 使用率很低.
  • Decision: It is justifiable to lower the accessibility level (adding one extra step for progressive disclosure) to declutter the primary interface.决策： 因此可以适当降低其入口层级，用多一步的渐进披露换取主界面的清晰。

Distinguishing by Static vs Dynamic用静态状态与动态过程区分

  • Policy (Static State): Represents ownership. It answers: "What am I covered for?" and "Is my protection valid?"保单（静态状态）： 代表用户拥有的保障，回答“我获得哪些保障？”以及“保障是否有效？”
  • Claim (Dynamic Process): Represents action. It answers: "Where is my money?" or "What is the status of my refund?"理赔（动态过程）： 代表正在进行的操作，回答“赔款到哪一步了？”或“退款状态如何？”

Potential Solution Impacts方案的潜在影响

*Note - As a new business line (Insurance), this project is prioritized for Q4 2025 implementation due to business focus on the purchase flow.*注：保险仍是新业务，团队当前优先关注购买流程，因此本项优化计划于 2025 年第四季度推进。

Reduce Operational Costs降低运营成本

  • Outcome: We anticipate a significant drop in users contacting support due to confusion about their claim status ("Where Is My Claim?").预期结果： 预期因不清楚理赔状态而联系客服的用户将明显减少。
  • Metric: Decrease in CS Ticket Volume (specifically claim-related inquiries).指标： 降低 客服工单量 （尤其是理赔相关咨询）。

Improve Wayfinding Efficiency提升导航效率

  • Outcome: Users should distinguish between Policy and Claim status faster and without mistakes.预期结果： 用户应能更快、更准确地区分保单与理赔状态。
  • Metric: Decrease in Navigation Error Rate (users clicking the wrong tab) and Task Completion Time.指标： 降低 导航错误率 （用户点错标签页）以及 任务完成时间.

Protect User Sentiment保护用户信任与留存

  • Outcome: A transparent post-purchase experience prevents user frustration, protecting long-term retention.预期结果： 透明的购买后体验能减少挫败，保护长期留存。
  • Metric: Maintain or Increase Post-Claim NPS (Net Promoter Score).指标： 维持或提升 理赔后 NPS （净推荐值）。
