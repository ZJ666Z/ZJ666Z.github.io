Login is frequently the first time a user interacts with a product, setting the tone for their entire experience. At Johnson Controls (JCI), while physical products shared a robust design language, the digital ecosystem remained fragmented.登录通常是用户与产品的第一次互动，也决定了后续体验的基调。Johnson Controls（JCI）的实体产品已有成熟的设计语言，但数字生态仍然割裂。

Problem: The Challenges & Strategic Trade-offs问题：挑战与策略取舍

Designing a global authentication framework required navigating complex business requirements and user experience friction.设计全球认证框架，需要同时处理复杂的业务要求与用户体验阻力。

01🛡️ Risk-based Access vs. Login Conversion🛡️ 风险分级访问 vs. 登录转化

Balancing enterprise-grade security with a high-success login experience requires intentional trade-offs.要同时满足企业级安全和高成功率登录体验，必须做出有意识的取舍。

Excessive Friction: When the login pattern includes too many fields, choices, or steps, users are more likely to hesitate or drop off.阻力过高： 登录流程中的字段、选项或步骤过多，会让用户犹豫甚至退出。

Inconsistent Patterns Across Apps: When products implement login and recovery differently, users see different steps, errors, and terminology.应用间模式不一致： 不同产品各自实现登录与找回流程，用户会面对不同的步骤、错误提示和术语。

→

Lower Completion Rates: Extra friction can reduce sign-in completion, especially for first-time or infrequent users.完成率下降： 额外阻力会降低登录完成率，尤其影响首次或低频用户。

More Password/Access Issues: More lockouts and resets can lead to more support requests and longer time to access the product.密码与访问问题增加： 更多锁定与重置问题会增加客服请求，也延长用户进入产品的时间。

02🧠 Path Discovery vs. Cognitive Load🧠 路径识别 vs. 认知负担

The primary entry point must help users reach the correct login path with minimal thinking and minimal errors.主入口需要用最少的判断成本，将用户带到正确的登录路径。

Ambiguous Entry Choices: Generic labels like “Sign in” and “Sign up,” or presenting multiple auth options at once, forces users to decide before they understand the context.入口选项含糊： “登录”“注册”等泛化标签，或一次展示过多认证选项，会迫使用户在尚未理解情境时先做选择。

Information Overload: Showing all fields and SSO options simultaneously increases scanning effort and makes the “right next step” unclear.信息过载： 同时展示全部字段与 SSO 选项会增加浏览负担，让正确的下一步变得不清楚。

→

Wrong-path Errors: Users choose the wrong method (JCI password vs. SSO), hit dead ends, and restart—driving frustration and abandonment.误入错误路径： 用户可能选错 JCI 密码或 SSO 路径，走到死路后重新开始，最终产生挫败并放弃。

Decision Delay: Extra thinking time slows the login journey and increases drop-off under time pressure.决策延迟： 额外思考会拖慢登录，在时间压力下进一步提高流失。

03🏷️ Multi-tenant Brand System vs. Governance🏷️ 多租户品牌表达 vs. 系统治理

A unified login pattern must scale across products, brands, and customers—without turning into a one-off factory.统一登录模式既要适配不同产品、品牌和客户，也不能沦为不断生产一次性方案的“定制工厂”。

One-size-fits-all Standardization: Forcing a single layout and visual treatment across all products can under-serve flagship experiences and customer expectations.一刀切的标准化： 强制所有产品使用完全相同的布局与视觉，会损害旗舰产品体验，也难以满足客户预期。

Unbounded Customization: Letting every team freely customize login creates design drift, longer QA cycles, and harder-to-maintain components.无边界定制： 允许每个团队自由定制，又会造成设计漂移、延长 QA 周期，并提高组件维护成本。

→

Inconsistent Brand Presence: The ecosystem feels fragmented; users may question legitimacy when the login experience looks different across products.品牌表达不一致： 割裂的登录体验会让生态显得不统一，甚至让用户怀疑页面是否可信。

Operational Overhead: Without clear guardrails (tokens, slots, ownership), customization becomes expensive and slows down product teams.运营负担： 如果没有 Token、插槽和所有权等明确边界，定制成本会持续上升，并拖慢产品团队。

01

🛡️ Risk-based Access vs. Login Conversion🛡️ 风险分级访问 vs. 登录转化

Centralized Login Page集中式登录页面

  • Target pattern: A centralized login entry point—customized by JCI, hosted by Okta—replaces app-owned login pages across the JCI ecosystem.目标模式： 以 JCI 定制、由 Okta 托管的集中式登录入口，替代 JCI 生态中各应用自建的登录页面。
  • Smart routing: Users are redirected from any app to the centralized Login Screen, then routed to either JCI username/password (no IdP on record) or the correct SSO Identity Provider (IdP found via email lookup).智能路由： 用户从任意应用进入集中式登录页；系统再根据邮箱查询结果，将其引导至 JCI 用户名/密码路径，或正确的 SSO 身份提供方。
  • Seamless return: After authentication (and optional account selection for multi-org users), users are redirected back to the originating app's Home Screen.无缝返回： 完成认证后（多组织用户可先选择账户），用户会被送回原应用首页。
  • Platform value: Centralization improves governance and consistency, reduces maintenance/security burden on product teams, and enables SSO ("log in once, access everywhere") with scalable theming for brands/customers.平台价值： 集中化提升了治理与一致性，减轻产品团队的维护和安全负担，同时支持“一次登录、处处访问”的 SSO，并为品牌与客户提供可扩展主题能力。

3.2 Flexible Authentication Architecture3.2 灵活的认证架构

To meet the diverse security and user experience needs across JCI's massive product portfolio, we defined three core authentication strategies within the design system:为满足 JCI 庞大产品组合中不同的安全与体验需求，我们在设计系统中定义了三类核心认证策略：

1. 🔐 Single Sign-On (SSO) — The ecosystem hub breaking down silos1. 🔐 单点登录（SSO）——打破孤岛的生态枢纽

  • Behavior/Interaction: Integrates Okta's SSO service to build a unified, enterprise-grade authentication portal.行为与交互： 集成 Okta SSO 服务，建立统一的企业级认证门户。
  • Design Value: Completely breaks down identity barriers between products. Consumers only need to authenticate once to seamlessly access multiple unrelated JCI products, eliminating the heavy burden of memorizing multiple credentials.设计价值： 打通产品间的身份壁垒。用户只需认证一次，即可无缝进入多个 JCI 产品，不必记忆多套凭证。

2. 🧠 Progressive Authentication — The standard for reducing cognitive load2. 🧠 渐进式认证——降低认知负担的标准方案

  • Behavior/Interaction: Requests only the User ID upfront, deferring the password input to the next step.行为与交互： 第一步只请求用户 ID，密码输入延后到下一步。
  • Design Value: Drastically reduces cognitive load by eliminating non-essential visual distractions. The system automatically determines the correct login path in the background, instead of forcing users to read through options and manually choose.设计价值： 去除非必要视觉干扰，大幅降低认知负担。系统在后台自动判断正确登录路径，无需用户阅读多组选项再手动选择。

3. ⚡ One-step Authentication — Maximized efficiency for high-frequency scenarios3. ⚡ 单步认证——为高频场景最大化效率

  • Behavior/Interaction: Displays both the User ID and password input fields on the same page, requiring no step-by-step navigation.行为与交互： 在同一页面展示用户 ID 与密码输入框，无需逐步跳转。
  • Design Value: Tailor-made for products that require high-frequency log-in and log-off actions. By avoiding unnecessary page refreshes and redirects, it provides the fastest possible entry experience for specific high-frequency business use cases.设计价值： 面向需要频繁登录与退出的产品，避免不必要的页面刷新和重定向，为特定高频业务提供最快的进入体验。
