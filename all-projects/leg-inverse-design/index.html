Robot lower leg: deep-learning inverse design and topology optimisation机器人小腿：深度学习逆向设计与拓扑优化

My quadruped walks on four printed lower legs, each turned by an SG90 knee servo. Inverse design with deep learning: a conditional neural implicit field turns performance targets into a printable leg in 2–5 seconds. It learned from 3D topology optimisation: 80 legs, each scored by finite elements.我的四足机器人靠 4 条打印小腿行走，每条由膝关节的 SG90 舵机驱动。用深度学习做逆向设计：条件神经隐式场由性能目标在 2–5 秒内生成可打印的腿。训练数据来自 3D 拓扑优化：80 条小腿，每条都经有限元评分。

Overview概览

The part: the robot’s lower leg优化对象：机器人的小腿

The quadruped has eight SG90 servos: four at the hips, four at the knees. Each knee servo turns one printed lower leg, R3, whose end meets the ground. This project redesigns that lower leg twice over: forward, by topology optimisation; inverse, by deep learning. The rest of the robot stays as it is.这台四足机器人有 8 个 SG90 舵机：4 个在髋关节，4 个在膝关节。每个膝关节舵机带动一条打印小腿 R3，小腿末端触地。本项目用两种方法重新设计这条小腿：正向用拓扑优化，逆向用深度学习；机器人其余部分不变。

Why redesign it为什么重新设计

Servo load舵机负载

Less load on plastic gears减轻塑料齿轮的负载

The knee SG90 has plastic gears. The lighter the leg and the closer its mass sits to the knee, the less torque the gears carry. Measures: mass and knee inertia ↓.膝关节 SG90 是塑料齿轮。小腿越轻、质量离膝越近，齿轮承受的力矩越小。指标：质量、绕膝转动惯量 ↓。

Structural margin结构余量

Stiffness for added payload为加装部件留出刚度

Upgrades will add parts, and the legs will carry more. The first round holds the original’s stiffness as a floor at the least mass; for more, the inverse model generates to the new targets: a leg with at least 1.95× stance and 1.80× push stiffness weighs 5.67 g, still lighter than the original.升级会加装更多部件，腿的载荷随之增加。第一轮以原版刚度为下限、质量最小；需要更高刚度时，逆向模型按新目标生成：站立 ≥ 1.95 倍、推地 ≥ 1.80 倍的腿为 5.67 g，仍比原版轻。

Grip抓地

More grip in the push direction提高推地方向的抓地

Grip is set by the sole’s shape and the floor, and finite elements cannot compute friction. The sole radius is a design condition; grip is measured in an A/B walk against the original on a wood floor, and the result becomes a target of the second round.抓地由脚底形状和地面决定，有限元无法计算摩擦。脚底半径是设计条件之一；抓地在木地板上与原版做 A/B 行走实测，实测结果作为第二轮的目标。

Constraints: knee torque no higher than the original’s, and a leg length of 47.1 ± 1 mm, so the firmware gait stays as it is.约束：膝关节扭矩不高于原版，腿长 47.1 ± 1 mm，固件步态无需重调。

Measures评价

How each measure is computed每项指标怎么计算

Mass and inertia质量与惯量

From the solid model按实心模型积分

Integrated over the part’s 0.4 mm voxels, PLA at 1.24 g/cm³; the inertia is taken about the knee axis.在零件的 0.4 mm 体素上积分，PLA 密度 1.24 g/cm³；转动惯量绕膝关节轴计算。

Stiffness and strength刚度与强度

Finite elements, five load cases有限元，五个工况

Four stance cases and the push, on the same grid as the original. Every leg has a safety factor of at least 12 where 3 is required, so strength never binds.四个站立工况和推地工况，与原版在同一网格上计算。所有腿安全系数 ≥ 12（要求 3），强度不构成限制。

Walking步行

Knee torque, foot lift, stride膝关节扭矩、抬脚高度、步长

The firmware’s six-frame gait on the robot’s kinematic chain; all three depend only on leg length and sole radius.由固件六帧步态在机器人运动链上计算；三者只取决于腿长和脚底半径。

Grip抓地

An A/B walk on the robot实机 A/B 行走

On a wood floor against the original legs: forward travel, sideways drift and turning per cycle.木地板上与原版腿对比：每个周期的前进、横移与转角。

Scoring评分方法

Every leg, the original included, is scored as a solid model, so print settings apply to all alike. The part is voxelised at 0.4 mm and solved with eight-node bricks carrying Wilson’s incompatible modes (PLA, E = 2750 MPa, ν = 0.35), fixed where the servo touches it.所有腿（含原版）按实心模型评分，打印参数对所有腿相同。零件以 0.4 mm 体素化，采用带 Wilson 非协调模式的八节点六面体单元求解（PLA，E = 2750 MPa，ν = 0.35），舵机接触面固定。

Stiffness is the load over the mean displacement along it. The stance score is the mean of the four stance ratios to the original; the push score is the push ratio. Strength is 35 MPa over the 99th-percentile von Mises stress across all cases.刚度 = 载荷 ÷ 载荷方向的平均位移。站立分数为四个站立工况相对原版比值的平均，推地分数为推地工况的比值。强度 = 35 MPa ÷ 全部工况第 99 百分位 von Mises 应力。

Method方法

From 19 parameters to 48,000 voxels从 19 个参数到 4.8 万个体素

01

Parametric: two rails beat the original参数化：两根杆件已优于原版

A 19-parameter family of branching members, 1,440 designs searched with NSGA-II: 2.3× the original’s stiffness at its mass, or 30 % lighter at its stiffness.19 参数的分叉杆件族，NSGA-II 评估 1,440 个设计：同质量下刚度为原版的 2.3 倍，同刚度下轻 30%。

02

2D free-form: the same result2D 自由形状：结果相同

A density per 0.6 mm cell of the side profile, extruded through the thickness. The optimiser again draws two rails; its front lies on the parametric one.侧面轮廓按 0.6 mm 网格分配密度，沿厚度拉伸。优化器同样得到两根杆件，前沿与参数化结果重合。

03

3D: the shank closes into a tube3D：小腿杆闭合成管

With the thickness free, 23 of the 79 legs close part of the shank into a ring, which resists bending and torsion in every direction, the push included.厚度方向放开后，79 条腿中有 23 条的小腿杆局部闭合为环形截面，可同时抵抗各方向的弯曲和扭转，包括推地方向。

The 3D optimiser3D 拓扑优化器

Variables设计变量

A density per voxel每个体素一个密度

Objective目标

Weighted compliance加权柔度

Each case’s compliance over the original’s; push weight w, each stance case (1 − w) / 4.各工况柔度除以原版柔度；推地权重 w，四个站立工况各 (1 − w) / 4。

Constraint约束

Mass budget质量预算

The budget sets the material fraction; keep-in cells are fixed solid, keep-out cells empty.由质量预算换算体积分数；保留区固定为实，禁区固定为空。

Filter and projection滤波与投影

Members of at least 2.4 mm杆件最小 2.4 mm

Density filter of 2.4 mm radius, empty outside the domain; tanh projection, β doubled every 10 iterations from the 20th, 1 → 16.密度滤波半径 2.4 mm，设计域外视为空；tanh 投影，β 自第 20 步起每 10 步加倍，1 → 16。

Update更新

SIMP with optimality criteriaSIMP + 最优性准则

Penalty 3, minimum stiffness 10⁻³, move limit 0.08, 80 iterations; one factorisation solves all five cases.惩罚指数 3，最小刚度 10⁻³，步长 0.08，80 步；一次分解求解五个工况。

Inverse model逆向模型

The inverse model in four steps逆向模型的四个步骤

01

Pick the conditions选条件

The eight Gaussian processes predict every measure, with its standard deviation σ, for 20,000 random candidates (sole radius sampled on a log scale).8 个高斯过程预测条件空间内 2 万个随机候选（脚底半径对数采样）的各项性能及其标准差 σ。

A candidate qualifies when μ − σ meets each minimum and μ + σ stays under each maximum; the lightest predicted mass wins.下限目标满足 μ − σ ≥ 目标、上限目标满足 μ + σ ≤ 目标即为合格；取预测质量最小者。

02

Generate the shape生成形状

A conditional neural implicit field gives the signed distance to the surface at any point, for the chosen conditions (below).条件神经隐式场：给定条件下，输出任意点到表面的符号距离（见下）。

03

Make the part成型

Decoded on 556,800 grid points and meshed; the surface is offset by bisection within ±0.4 mm until the mass matches the surrogate’s prediction within 0.5 %. Without this, light legs come out 5 % thin and 20 % soft.在 556,800 个网格点上解码并提取表面；表面在 ±0.4 mm 内二分偏移，直到质量与代理模型预测相差 < 0.5%。不做这一步时，轻腿偏薄 5%、刚度偏低 20%。

Finishing as in forward design: Taubin smoothing, exact servo interfaces.后处理与正向设计相同：Taubin 平滑，精确切出舵机接口。

Check复核

The neural implicit field神经隐式场

The shape is a signed distance field f(p, c): p is a point in space, c the four conditions, and the output is p’s distance to the surface, negative inside. The surface is the level set f = 0. One network holds every design: shapes between the optimised conditions are its interpolation, and the decoding grid sets the resolution.形状用符号距离场 f(p, c) 表示：p 为空间中的点，c 为 4 个条件，输出 p 到表面的距离，内部为负；表面是 f = 0 的等值面。一个网络表示全部设计：优化样本之间的形状由网络插值得到，分辨率由解码网格决定。

Input: the point, its sines and cosines at six frequencies (2ᵏπ, k = 0…5) so the network can draw thin members, and the four scaled conditions: 43 numbers. Six layers of 256 with SiLU; the 43 inputs join again at the fourth layer. Output: the distance, clipped at ±4 mm. 351,489 trained weights.输入：点坐标、6 个频率（2ᵏπ，k = 0…5）的正余弦编码（使网络能表达细杆件）和 4 个归一化条件，共 43 维。6 层 × 256，SiLU 激活；43 维输入在第 4 层再次接入。输出：截断于 ±4 mm 的距离。共 351,489 个训练参数。

Twenty target sets20 组目标

Twenty target sets, spread evenly: stance 1–2×, push 1–2.5×, knee torque at most 8–16 N·mm, leg 46.1–48.1 mm. All twenty were met, seventeen at the first try. Every generated leg is lighter than the lightest dataset leg meeting the same targets (median −7 %, −1 % to −23 %): the model finds designs between the optimisation samples.20 组目标均匀分布：站立 1–2 倍，推地 1–2.5 倍，膝关节扭矩上限 8–16 N·mm，腿长 46.1–48.1 mm。20 组全部达标，17 组一次达标。每条生成的腿都比数据集中满足相同目标的最轻设计更轻（中位数 −7%，−1% 至 −23%）：模型生成的是优化样本之间的设计。
