A LITTLE ABOUT ME:

I am a product designer with experience in E-commerce, Fintech, and Design Infrastructure, turning data + intuition into measurable product growth, currently working at TikTok.

At TikTok, I own the end-to-end UX for the Hybrid Cashier and Refund & Withdraw Cashier product lines. As the main designer for Hybrid Cashier, I am reshaping the design workflow around AI — structuring product knowledge, standardizing design decisions, accelerating delivery, and extending design output into PR-ready code.

Previously, I optimized TikTok Shop's insurance experience across five markets across SEA (PH, ID, MY, TH) & US. By auditing UX gaps that affected Gross Written Premium (GWP) and Net Promoter Score (NPS), I proposed design improvements to reduce drop-offs and increase adoption.

At Johnson Controls, I co-developed the Global Element Design System, adopted by 34 design and 2 engineering teams worldwide. This system cut component creation time by 42%, improved design token efficiency by 65%, reduced design maintenance by 62%, and generated over $1M in annual efficiency gains.

I combine creativity with data-driven insights to design scalable products, systems, and AI-enabled workflows that improve usability, consistency, and business outcomes.
