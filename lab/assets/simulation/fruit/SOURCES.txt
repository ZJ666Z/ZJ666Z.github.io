strawberry.glb selects the largest whole berry, calyx and stalk from the group;
the original PBR textures, UVs and normals are preserved.
Reproduce with python3 scripts/prepare_strawberry.py.
The Three.js viewer rotates and scales this model to a 4.5 cm visual cue.
It is not a collider. The original food sensing radius and physics are unchanged.
